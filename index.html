What We Do

Equity Ownership

Lelylaan holds equity stakes in a small group of separately incorporated AI-software and technology companies within the same ownership group.

Strategic Support

We provide strategic input across the group, helping each company think through direction, governance, and long-term planning.

Operational Support

We offer practical operational support, such as finance, administration, and shared services, to companies within the group.

Approach

Independently Incorporated

Each venture is its own separately incorporated company, operating under its own name and management.

Shared Operational Discipline

Companies within the group draw on common standards and practices for how they run their operations.

Long-Term Ownership

Lelylaan takes a patient, long-term view of the companies it holds equity in, rather than seeking short-term returns.
